# Brand

- Category: "AI Programming Engine" / "Intelligent Program Design". Not software, an app, or a platform. (GTM v1.0)
- Guiding line: "AI is the tool. The coach's expertise is the product." (Roadmap v2)
- Primary promise: "Stop writing programs. Start coaching."
- Site headline: "Elite-Level, Individualized Programs Built in Seconds. Engineered for modern strength coaches. Scale your expertise without burning out."
- Never position as a coach replacement, a workout logger (TrainHeroic), a template library, or an athlete-management tool (Teamworks).
- AI skepticism rule: every AI mention immediately says what it does for the coach.
- Ryne's quality bar (2026-09-04): the best S&C program design product on the market, and ship to users soon with bugs mostly gone.
- Look: dark charcoal/black with electric blue. Pull exact hex, type, and UI from ironblueprint.app before any ad, graphic, or post. Do not invent a palette.
- Do not mix Achieve gym copy with IronBlueprint coach copy.
- Instagram: @iron_blueprint. Ryne's X: @RGioviano. The site does not link to X.
