# Cold email reply rules (Lavender, adapted)

Source: Lavender cold-email findings (Nick Abraham / X, locked for Sales 2026-10-24). Apply to **lead first-touch** and **re-engage** emails only. Do not invent client stories, prices, or results.

## Length and scan

1. Keep under about 75 words; aim for 25 to 50 when the ask is simple.
2. Write at about a 3rd to 5th grade reading level (short words, short sentences).
3. Use 1 to 2 sentence paragraphs (most opens are on a phone).

## Opening

4. Lead with a real observation from their form, sport, age, or prior contact. Never invent one.
5. Turn that observation into a short hypothesis tied to the problem you solve (strength, speed, power, fewer injuries, adult strength / stay active).
6. Double-check every fact before the draft goes out.
7. Focus on their problem or goal, not the facility.

## Proof and tone

8. Use a customer story only when it is real and similar (sport, age, goal). Skip the story if you do not have one.
9. Drop did-you-know stats and informative lecture tones.
10. Cut cliches and soft openers (hope this finds you well, just following up).
11. Light hedge words (might, could) only on cold guesses. Never hedge when answering a direct inbound question: answer first with concrete specifics.

## Ask and subject

12. The goal of a cold or re-engage email is a reply. Make one ask only.
13. Make the question easy to answer (days and times, yes or no, pick a window). Skip open-ended essay questions.
14. Still end with a question plus open time windows when booking is the next step (product lock wins over never ask for a meeting).
15. Subject lines: 1 to 3 words, sound internal. For cold subjects avoid verbs, numbers, questions, and first names when that fits the thread.

## Delivery

16. Leave out images and links in cold and re-engage emails (deliverability).
17. Space sends; do not blast high volume.

## Locks that always win

- Answer first on inbound questions; cut soft openers; concrete specifics; end with a question plus open time windows.
- Achieve lead emails may include one Pricing starts at line only when the price was read live that day from the locked source.
- No prices in ads.
- Never invent client stories or results.
- Pre-draft check before any draft goes to Ryne.
