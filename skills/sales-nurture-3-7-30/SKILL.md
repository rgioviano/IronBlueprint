# Sales nurture: 3-7-30 Method

Standing guidance for IronBlueprint sales follow-up and email nurture (Sales owns; Marketing owns paid nurture only). Drafts only until Ryne reviews.

## Mindset

The person who asks the most times gets the most — but people don’t like being asked, so give value consistently to earn the right to keep asking. Winners take more shots because they get into scoring position first.

Think **value sequence**, not “follow-up.” Most businesses follow up 1–2× then stop and lose half+ of sales. Don’t just say “buy” over and over.

## The sequence

### First 3 days
- **Day 1:** Best case study
- **Day 2:** Best client transformation
- **Day 3:** Biggest industry insight

### Next 7 days
One touch every other day. Each removes **ONE** specific objection (reverse FAQ with proof woven in).

### Next 30 days
Two touches/week. Alternate success stories and industry insights. Each touch must be valuable enough to stand alone even if they never buy.

## Rule for every touch

Make them think “this is good even if I don’t buy.”

## How it fits

- Use this frame for IronBlueprint lead nurture that is not already locked.
- Do not invent prices, names, or availability. No prices in ads or inbound replies.
