# Achieve writing style

Use this when drafting Achieve or IronBlueprint email, or Numa business texts, and again after Ryne sends so the next draft matches him more closely.

This file is living. After every send, rewrite the Learned section from the sent-vs-draft diff. Do not leave new voice habits only in chat memory.

## After he sends (required)

1. Pull the sent body (Gmail sent on that thread, or his outbound Numa bubble). Do not send, create drafts, or type into a composer.
2. Diff it against the draft that was in chat.
3. Keep only durable voice changes: greeting, length, what he cut, words he added, policy firmness, and closer. Ignore one-off facts.
4. **Edit this skill** - add or revise a dated bullet under Learned. If a new habit contradicts an old one, replace the old line. Then use the updated file on the next draft.
5. Do not restating the same style lecture in chat. Stay quiet unless a pattern is worth locking.

## Gym email (Achieve / IronBlueprint)

- Greeting: `Hi FirstName,` never Hey, Dear, or hope-this-finds-you. Short follow-ups can drop Hi and use `FirstName,` only, or no greeting at all on a one-line confirm.
- 2-4 short paragraphs. Answer in the first sentence. One reason or number. One next step.
- Closer: `Thanks,` then the CSCS signature as **plain text only**. No hyperlinks, no `https://`, no markdown links. Gmail tracking URLs mess up his signature. He sometimes writes `Thanks!` on a warm close; do not force the bang. One-line slot confirms can skip Thanks entirely.
- Write the signature exactly as lines of text:

```
Ryne Gioviano, MSEd, CSCS
Owner, Achieve Sports Performance & Personal Training

For scheduling or account questions:
Contact@achieve-personaltraining.com | Text: 630.340.4114
```

- **Connector / API sends (2026-09-05):** omit the `www.achievesportsperformance.com` line. Gmail wraps any URL into a `google.com/url?...` tracking link even on plain-text sends. Contact@ and the phone stay clean. Site stays on the live web signature only - never put a URL or www line in connector-sent bodies.

- On contact@ threads that already close with `The Achieve Team` plus the website line, match that instead of CSCS.
- We / athlete-first. Warm-but-direct coach. Sorry to hear that / Sorry for the later reply when it fits - but drop "Sorry to hear that" once the account change is already done, and do not open a cancel/leave ask with it.
- Policy once, no apology theater. Scheduling: one or two concrete times. If they already named a window, pick the slot yourself.
- No emojis, circling back, or corporate softeners.
- No em dashes (-). Use periods, commas, or parentheses.
- Cancel/refund emails he owns: he often BCC `kara@achieve-personaltraining.com` so she can run the account change. Do not BCC until he says send, and only if that is how he sent the last similar one. A contract-hold to the parent may go CC instead.

## Numa texts

- Always greet first: `Hi, FirstName.` (comma after Hi, period after the name). Never skip the greeting, even mid-thread.
- 1-3 sentences after the greeting. Logistics first. Almost never `Thanks,` / `Ryne`.
- We for the business. No gym-email structure and no signature.

### SCDD Instant Form first text (locked)

When a new Short Course Dryland Domination Instant Form hits, do not compose from scratch. Draft this exact text (30-min watch) in the Administrative group. Merge the form submitter's first name (parent if both exist, else the one name). Never leave a placeholder. No $349, no bullets, no extra sentences, no rewriting. If the form is not SCDD, do not use this. Drafts only until Ryne says send.

```
Hi, [Name]. It's Ryne at Achieve Sports Performance. I just saw your info from the Short Course Dryland form. First step is a 1:1 eval so we can completely individualize the 6-week program. What are some afternoon days/times that work this week or next?
```

Later replies in that thread go back to the normal Numa greeting.

## Home / personal email

- Different voice. I-not-we. Shorter. `Thanks Ryne`. Do not use gym voice at home.

## Do not invent

Prices, refund amounts, availability, or commitments he has not stated. If a fact is missing, flag it outside the draft fence.
