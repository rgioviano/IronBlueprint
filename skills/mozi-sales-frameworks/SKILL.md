# Mozi frameworks (Achieve + IronBlueprint)

Standing sales defaults locked 2026-09-08 alongside [Sales nurture 3-7-30](sand-workflow:sales-nurture-3-7-30). Drafts only until Ryne reviews. SCDD Instant Form / E1-E4 product locks still win on those sends.

## Sales owns / co-owns

### 1. Speed to Contact (≤60 sec)
Sales leads live human contact. Marketing owns the form trigger. Measure first-touch in **seconds**, not hours.

### 2. 9-Word Cash Email
Marketing sends. **Sales owns reply handling** the moment they answer: “Are you still looking to [goal]?”

### 3. Close-Rate Pricing Diagnostic
Sales leads. Close rate is the price signal:
- **80%+** → raise hard
- **Under 30%** → sell better first (don’t cut price first)
Marketing helps packaging.

### 4. Four-Part Persuasion (verbal / calls)
- Act → add good / remove bad
- Don’t → add bad / remove good
- Make it **immediate**

### 5. Pre-call leverage / VSL (locked 2026-09-17)

Long calls, too many touches to close, and price-only comparison = **low leverage**. Fix: answer every buyer question *before* the live conversation.

**Questions to pre-answer**
- Why buy
- Why buy from you
- What specifically happens
- What results to expect

**Biggest move:** make them watch a video (VSL / pre-call film) before they talk to you or a rep. Perfect presentation that pitches and positions vs competitors.

**What the video must tell them**
1. What’s in it for them
2. The good they’re going to get
3. The bad they’re going to avoid
4. Proof of why they should listen
5. Your specific mechanism that’s different than everyone else’s
6. Every common sales-call objection, one by one: why it’s wrong, what’s actually right, proof
7. Finish with fast-hitting proof and case studies

**Call gate**
- Ask if they watched before you talk.
- If not: have them watch in the first 10-15 minutes. Offer to get them a coffee while they do.
- Position: “This saves us an hour or more if you do it in 15 minutes.” People give time when it saves more.

**Sandwich (still stands):** ≤5 min intro → best VSL (or force-watch if they skipped) → decision call. Marketing builds 3-5 VSLs by avatar; Sales owns the gate, script, and objection list that feeds the film.

### 6. Belief-Breaking Formula (calls)
1. Wrong belief (1st person)
2. Why it’s wrong
3. What’s right
4. Similar-avatar proof  
Use on DIY gym / spreadsheet objections (Achieve parents; IronBlueprint coaches). Same skeleton feeds VSL objection blocks.

### 7-9. Marketing-primary (support when asked)
Checkbox offer, pain-hook ads, branded search. Sales supports when pulled in.

### 8. Pain-Hook (Sales assist)
Help capture worst-moment clips at peak emotion for Marketing to use.

### 10. Partner Giveaway Offer (Sales / BD)
Give partners a sellable free product **they keep 100%**. You upsell the end customer.
- Achieve: chiro / PT / schools
- IronBlueprint: coaches / agencies
