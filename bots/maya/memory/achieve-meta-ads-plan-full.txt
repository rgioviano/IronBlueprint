1. The machine
Campaign setup
Objective: Leads (Instant Forms) every month.
Campaign budget optimization on. Daily budget $16.40. Do not change budget more than 20% inside 72 hours.
Two ad sets most months: Cold and Warm. October and August add a third set only when the chapter says so.
Three ads in the main cold set on Day 1. One or two in Warm (last month’s winner plus this month’s package ad).
Advantage+ audience on, location locked. If spend hits 18-year-olds with no kids, switch that set to manual.
Location
People living in or recently in a 18-mile radius of the facility for parent campaigns; 15-mile radius for adult campaigns. Cities: Aurora, North Aurora, Batavia, Geneva, St. Charles, Naperville, Oswego, Montgomery, Sugar Grove, Yorkville, Plainfield, Warrenville, Lisle.
Placements and tracking
Advantage+ placements. Turn off Audience Network if it wastes spend. If Stories buy 3-second views and no form opens, restrict to Feeds + Reels.
Pixel + Conversions API live before October spend. Events: ViewContent, Lead, Schedule, Purchase.
UTM: utm_source=meta&utm_medium=paid&utm_campaign=YYYYMM_theme&utm_content=adA|adB|adC.
Creative rules
Film in the gym. 9:16 first. Words on screen in the first two seconds.
One promise per ad. Permission on every athlete face.
Refresh a creative when cold frequency hits 3.5 and CTR is falling.
30-day test loop
Days
Do this
1–3
Launch three ads. Even spend. Do not edit copy or targeting.
4–7
Read form opens and CTR. Kill an ad if CTR is under 0.8% and cost per form open is more than 2× the set average.
8
Keep the winner. Add one challenger — new first line, same package.
9–21
70% winner / 30% challenger. Swap if the challenger wins CPL four days straight.
22–30
80% winner / 20% warm. Next month’s first lines come from this month’s comments.
Anytime
Kill if CPL is more than 2× the month target after 8 leads, or that ad produces 5 no-shows in a row.

Day 14 and 30: recycle into the warm set.
